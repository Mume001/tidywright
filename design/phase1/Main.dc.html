Questions agencies ask

Does it slow my site down?
No. A 4 KB script and an iframe that loads after your page. Nothing runs on your page itself.
Can I edit the fixes before the visitor sees them?
Not in the free widget; the report is instant. On paid plans you can add your own intro and CTA. Editing per report is coming with the site connection features.
Is it GDPR compliant?
The visitor ticks a consent box whose text and time are stored with the lead. You are the controller, we are the processor, and the DPA is part of the terms.
What sites can it check?
Any public page. Sites behind heavy bot protection can refuse the check; the visitor is told and you are notified.
What about JavaScript-only sites?
We detect them and render the page in a real browser before checking.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, from the billing page. You keep the paid plan until the end of the period, then drop to Free without losing leads for 30 days.
